About

A delivery leader who still recruits

Arunpratap is a results-driven Recruitment Leader with 9+ years of 360° IT recruitment across the UK, Europe, US, India and GCC markets — spanning contract and permanent hiring for technology, financial services, Big 4 consulting, SaaS and enterprise environments.

At IMS People Possible, he built the recruitment function from a single seat to a team of 15+, turning new joiners into live-billing consultants through sourcing standards, screening frameworks, pipeline reviews and placement-focused coaching.

He partners with C-suite leaders, Heads of Engineering and Talent Acquisition teams at global financial institutions, Big 4 firms and product organisations — and stays hands-on across the full cycle, from requisition intake to offer negotiation and onboarding.

Fluent in the contract models that matter to each market: UK Inside and Outside IR35, B2B and Umbrella; US W2, C2C and 1099; EU contract and permanent; and GCC direct-hire and EOR models.
